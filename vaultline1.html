Quick Start
Password Check

Has this password appeared in a leak?

This check uses k-anonymity — your full password never leaves your device. Only the first 5 characters of a partial hash are sent to HaveIBeenPwned.

Type to see strength
8+ chars Uppercase Number Symbol
Tip: think twice before typing your real password anywhere — this page is safe, but it is a good habit to avoid entering real passwords on unknown sites.
Generate a Strong Password

Link Check

Is this link safe to open?

Honest note: this is not a live global threat database. This scans the URL structure for known phishing patterns — fake domains, IP-based links, brand-name tricks, suspicious TLDs, etc.

QR Check

Check a QR code before scanning

UPI payment QR scams are very common — fake QR codes make you send money instead of receiving. Upload a QR photo and we will decode the hidden link/data and analyze it.

Screenshot or photo both work — everything is processed inside your browser, the image is never uploaded anywhere.
File Check

Is this filename suspicious?

Attackers disguise files — like "Invoice.pdf.exe" which looks like a PDF but is actually a program. Paste the filename you received on WhatsApp/email here.

UPI Check

Is this UPI ID or link safe?

Scammers send fake UPI payment requests or QR codes. Paste a UPI ID (like name@upi) or a UPI pay link here to verify its structure and spot red flags.

SMS Shield

Scan a suspicious SMS

Paste the full SMS text you received. We will scan for phishing keywords, fake urgency, suspicious links, and known scam patterns used in India and globally.

Email Breach

Has your email been in a data breach?

We check against HaveIBeenPwned using their secure API. Your email address is hashed before sending — the full email never leaves your device.

This uses k-anonymity. Only a partial hash of your email is sent to the API.
URL Expand

Expand a shortened URL

Shortened links (bit.ly, t.co, tinyurl, etc.) can hide malicious destinations. We try to resolve the final destination without actually visiting it, and show you what is behind the link.

File Hash

Calculate file hash (SHA-256)

Upload any file to compute its SHA-256 hash. You can compare this hash with the one published by the software vendor to verify the file has not been tampered with.

The file is hashed locally in your browser. It is never uploaded to any server.
EXIF Stripper

Strip metadata from images

Photos often contain hidden metadata (GPS location, camera model, date, etc.). Upload an image to remove all EXIF data and download a clean version.

Processing happens entirely in your browser. The cleaned image is downloaded automatically.
Privacy Scan

What does this browser reveal?

We show you what information websites can see about your browser and device. No data is sent anywhere — this is all read locally.

Emergency Kit

I think I have been scammed

Take a deep breath. Here is a step-by-step guide on what to do immediately if you suspect a scam, fraud, or account compromise.

1

Stop all communication

Do not reply to the scammer. Do not click any more links. Disconnect from the internet if malware is suspected. Take screenshots of everything as evidence.

2

Change passwords immediately

Change passwords for all critical accounts — email, bank, UPI apps, social media. Enable 2FA everywhere. Use a password manager if possible.

3

Report to your bank

Call your bank's fraud helpline immediately. In India, call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) or report at cybercrime.gov.in. For UPI fraud, use the app's dispute feature.

4

Scan your device

Run a full antivirus scan. Check for unknown apps or accessibility services enabled without your knowledge. Factory reset if necessary after backing up data.

5

Monitor and alert

Watch bank statements for 30 days. Set up transaction alerts. Inform friends/family if the scammer may contact them using your identity.

Understand the threats

Short guides on common digital threats and how to protect yourself against them.

Phishing
Fake emails, SMS, and websites designed to steal your login credentials or personal information.
QR Code Scams
Fake QR codes placed over real ones, or sent via WhatsApp, that redirect payments to scammers.
UPI Fraud
Scammers trick you into "receiving" money by asking you to scan a QR or enter your UPI PIN.
Social Engineering
Manipulating people into breaking security procedures through psychological tricks and fake urgency.
Malware
Viruses, trojans, ransomware, and spyware that infect your device to steal data or lock your files.
Identity Theft
Using stolen personal information to open accounts, take loans, or commit fraud in your name.

Privacy Policy

Vaultline is designed with privacy at its core. This policy explains what data we collect (spoiler: almost nothing) and how we handle it.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

1. Our Privacy-First Promise

Vaultline does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server. All security checks run entirely within your web browser. Your passwords, files, images, SMS texts, emails, and UPI IDs are processed locally and never leave your device.

2. What Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Passwords entered in the Password Check tool
  • URLs entered in the Link Check tool
  • Images uploaded to the QR Check or EXIF Stripper
  • Files uploaded to the File Hash tool
  • SMS texts scanned by SMS Shield
  • Email addresses checked for breaches
  • UPI IDs or payment links
  • TOTP secret keys
  • Any content of your Secure Notes

We physically cannot access this data because it never leaves your browser.

3. Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to save:

  • Your security checklist progress
  • Your activity history (check type and result only — not the actual input)
  • Your secure notes (stored unencrypted in your browser)
  • Your language preference

This data stays on your device only. It is not synced to any cloud or server. If you clear your browser data or use private/incognito mode, this data will be lost.

4. Third-Party APIs

For Password Check and Email Breach Check, we use the HaveIBeenPwned API (by Troy Hunt). Here's how it works:

  • Your password or email is hashed using SHA-1 inside your browser
  • Only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent to HaveIBeenPwned
  • The full password or email never leaves your device
  • This is called "k-anonymity" and is an industry-standard privacy technique

We also load the jsQR library from CDNJS for QR code decoding, and Google Fonts for typography.

5. Cookies

Vaultline does not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking scripts.

6. Security

While we implement security best practices (CSP headers, XSS protection, input sanitization), no website can guarantee 100% security. Use this Service at your own risk and always verify critical security decisions with professionals.
